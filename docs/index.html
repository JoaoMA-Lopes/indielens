Multi-language website support

🧮 Personalized Weighting System

Each rating is weighted using three factors:

  • Profile Match: How similar is this game to your library? (tags, genres, developer)
  • Engagement: How much have you played? (hours + achievement completion)
  • Soft Penalty: Are you achievement-focused or casual? (APH comparison)

Result: Your expertise areas get higher weight, ensuring recommendations match YOUR gaming style!
