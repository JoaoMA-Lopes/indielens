IndieLens: Personalized Game Discovery with Chrome Built-in AI

IndieLens solves the problem of discovering quality indie games tailored to individual gaming preferences. Unlike traditional one-size-fits-all ratings, IndieLens uses sophisticated mathematical algorithms to personalize every rating based on your Steam gaming history.

THE WEIGHTING SYSTEM: Your raw 0-100 rating is transformed using W = ProfileMatch × Engagement × SoftPenaltyAPH.
